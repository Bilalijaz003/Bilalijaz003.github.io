Real-time Object Detection

A desktop app that spots and labels everyday objects as they appear on a webcam, in a video or in a photo. It draws a box around each one with its name and confidence, counts what it sees, and can follow each object between frames with its own ID.

  • Recognizes 80 kinds of objects, from people and cars to phones and bottles
  • Live FPS and per-object counts on screen
  • Records annotated video, saves snapshots, filters to chosen classes
  • Python
  • YOLOv8
  • OpenCV
  • ByteTrack

CropGuard AI

A web app that helps farmers check crop health from a single leaf photo. Upload a wheat or sugarcane image and it reports whether the plant is healthy or names the disease, with a confidence score and practical advice on what to do next.

  • Identifies 6 conditions, including brown rust, yellow rust and red rot
  • Transfer-learned MobileNetV2 model served by a Flask API
  • Dashboard with scan history and healthy vs diseased statistics
  • TensorFlow
  • Keras
  • MobileNetV2
  • Flask
  • JavaScript
